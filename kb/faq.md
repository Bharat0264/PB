# PrintBee FAQ (Buzz reads this file)

## How PrintBee works
PrintBee is a student-friendly online printing service. Upload your files,
choose your print options, review the price, pay online, and we print,
pack and deliver your order. You can track it live in Orders.

## Supported files
- Formats: PDF, DOCX, JPG/JPEG, PNG.
- Limits: up to 50 MB per file, up to 100 files per order, up to 10,000 pages per PDF.
- Tip: use PDF for the most accurate result. DOCX layouts can shift
  slightly between devices, so check the preview before paying.

## Print options
Colour or black and white, single or double-sided, paper size, number of
copies, page ranges and binding. The price updates as you choose.

## Delivery code (OTP)
A 4-digit delivery code appears in your Tracking page once your order is out
for delivery. Share it with the Bee Runner ONLY when you have your order in
your hands. PrintBee staff and Buzz will never ask you for this code.

## Refunds and cancellations (DRAFT: owner to confirm)
- Cancel before printing starts: full refund.
- Cancel after printing has started: not refundable, because the order is
  custom-printed for you. Exception: see "Our mistake" below.
- Our mistake (wrong print, poor print quality, missing pages, damaged order):
  we reprint for free or refund you. Report within 24 hours of delivery and
  include a photo of the problem.
- Not delivered, or we could not deliver it: full refund.
- Your mistake (wrong file, wrong options, typo, layout differences): not
  refundable, so please review the preview before paying.
- Refund method: back to the original payment method.
- Refund time: TODO days after approval. Banks and UPI apps often take a few
  more days to show it.
- Only the PrintBee team can approve refunds. Buzz must never promise a
  refund. Buzz collects the details and creates a ticket.

## Payment problems
- Money deducted but the order is not confirmed: please wait a few minutes,
  then check Orders. Failed payments are usually reversed automatically by
  your bank within 5-7 working days (TODO: confirm). If it is not reversed,
  send us the payment ID or a screenshot and we will check.
- Never share card details, UPI PIN or OTPs in chat.

## Order issues
Late order, wrong print, missing pages or a Bee Runner problem: Buzz collects
the details and creates a support ticket. You can follow it in Help and tickets.
