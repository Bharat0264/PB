## How it works
Upload your files, choose your print options (colour or black and white, single or double sided, paper size, binding, copies), pay, and a Bee Runner brings the printed order to you.
The steps are: pay, we print, we pack, a Bee Runner picks up and delivers. You can follow each step on the Orders page.

## Files and limits
PrintBee accepts PDF files, and common document and image files that can be turned into pages.
A single file can be up to 50 MB and up to 10000 pages. One order can have up to 100 files.
You can pick a page range, mark which pages are colour while the rest stay black and white, choose Portrait or Landscape, and set copies from 1 to 100.
Paper sizes available on the site are shown on the pricing page.

## Colour, double sided and binding
Black and white and colour have different per-page prices. Double sided pages cost less per page than single sided on most rates.
Binding options are listed on the pricing page. Binding is charged per copy.

## Pricing explanation
Your total is the printing cost plus binding, plus packing, platform and delivery fees if the shop has set them, and tax if it applies.
The exact numbers come from the live pricing on the site. You always see the final total at checkout before you pay.
Coupons can be applied at checkout.

## Delivery code (OTP)
When your order is out for delivery, your Orders and tracking page shows a delivery code.
Give the code to your Bee Runner only when your order is in your hands. Do not share it earlier, and do not type it into chat.
If you cannot see a code, open the tracking page while the order is out for delivery. Support can help if it is missing.
PrintBee staff never ask you for the code.

## Payment problems
If money was taken but the order shows awaiting payment, wait a few minutes and refresh the Orders page.
If it still shows awaiting payment, create a support ticket with the order number and the time of the payment.
Never share card numbers, UPI PINs or bank passwords with anyone, including Buzz.

## Print quality problems
If a page is faded, missing or wrongly printed, open the order, tap Need help, and describe which pages are affected.
The warehouse team can see your print options and will fix or reprint where it is our fault.

If your Bee Runner is late, cannot find you, or something is wrong with the delivery, create a ticket linked to the order.
